Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for Mistfall Hunter Classes, a fan-made guide and build planner site.

Privacy

This site is designed as a static fan guide. The build planner runs in your browser and does not require an account. If analytics or hosting logs are enabled by a future deployment provider, they should be used only for site reliability and aggregate usage review.

Advertising and cookies

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is added after publication, third-party vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads. Any publisher ID and ads.txt entry should be added only after the real AdSense account is available; this site does not invent or publish a placeholder advertising seller ID.

Planner data

The class planner works locally in the browser. Your selected run format, combat rhythm, risk tolerance, and experience level are used to generate the visible recommendation and are not submitted to a server by this static build.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@mistfallhunterclasses.blog.
